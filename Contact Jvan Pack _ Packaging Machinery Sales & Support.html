Factory and quality

When you will hear back

When buyers write to a supplier in another country, the worry is rarely the price. It is silence. We usually reply within one working day, counted on China Standard Time (UTC+8) from the time we receive your message. The same standard applies to every route on this page. If we need more from you, the reply says exactly what is missing.

RouteWhat the first reply contains
QuoteFollow-up questions, or a suggested configuration with an indicative price. A formal quotation follows once the specification is agreed
Fault or serviceWhat the engineer can see in your video, what is still needed and the next step, with its cost where there is one
Spare partsThe part, the price and the shipping options. Availability is confirmed in each quotation
Installation and trainingWhich option fits your site: remote start-up, training at the factory test or an engineer on site
Factory visitProposed dates and the meeting point, and the date of your machine test if one is scheduled
DocumentsThe documents that exist for your machine type, or a plain statement that none does

What we do not promise. We do not publish a repair time. A fault that shows clearly on video is a different job from one that does not, and a figure given before we see the machine would be a guess. When a site visit is the better answer, the scope, dates and costs are agreed in writing before anyone travels.

Visit the factory, audit us or meet the team

Many buyers want to see who they are dealing with before they order. There are four ways to do it.

In person at the factory.

Watch your own machine being tested, walk the workshop and meet the engineers. We can set your visit around the test date.

By video call.

If travel is not practical, we can walk you through the workshop and the assembly area, or run your machine test live so you can ask for adjustments while the machine is still on our floor.

With your own inspector.

If your company appoints an inspection agency, tell us who is coming and what they need to see. We prepare the documents in advance.

At an exhibition.

Send the show name, the day you plan to attend and the machine or process you want to discuss. We will tell you whether the team will be there.

Questions before you write

 

See all FAQs

What is the best way to reach Jvan Pack?

It depends on the request. For a price, use the quote form. For a stopped machine, send the model, the serial number and a short video through the form or on WhatsApp. For a visit or a question, use the form in Section 3.

Is WhatsApp a proper way to reach you?

Yes. The WhatsApp number is the same as the telephone number, +86-183-5875-6313, and messages go to our team in Wenzhou. For formal requests and contracts, also write by email, so both sides have a written record.

How soon will I hear back?

Usually within one working day, on China Standard Time. The reply times section has the details and a time-difference table.

Do you have a partner in my country?

In Thailand, Greece, Slovakia, Chile, Brazil and South Korea, an independent local partner can help in your language and time zone. Everywhere else, you work directly with our team in Wenzhou.

Can I visit the factory before ordering?

Yes, whether you are attending the test of your own machine or assessing us before you commit. Write first, so we can confirm the dates and the meeting point.

Who do I contact about a machine fault?

Send the model, the serial number from the nameplate and a short video of the fault, with the product that was running. A video helps more than a description, because the engineer sees the fault instead of interpreting it.

How do I order spare parts?

Send the machine model, the serial number and a photo of the part. We reply with the part, the price and the shipping options. Start on the spare parts page.

How do I get CE certificates?

Copies are available on request. The six certificates cover our filling, capping and labeling machines only, so ask which documents exist for any other machine type.

Is my information kept private?

We use what you send to respond to your request, and we do not sell, rent or lease personal information. If you need a confidentiality agreement before sharing drawings, say so first.
